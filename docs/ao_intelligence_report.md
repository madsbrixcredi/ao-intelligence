## Unexpected findings

- Several activity office signals require canonicalization because raw DA_data fields do not always directly store the declaration accounting-office CVR.
- Some active accounting offices have high activity but low declaration linkage confidence when the derived CVR does not appear in declarations.
- There are declaration-heavy accounting offices with little or no observed DA_data activity, creating large untapped-potential estimates.

## Data quality observations

- DA_data rows processed: 6,927.
- User names were enriched for 6,112 activity rows via `Priority clients active compani`.
- Activity rows matched to declaration CVRs: 6,555; unmatched/candidate-only rows: 372.
- Office and department are not available for the main DA_data activity feed; office/department reporting should not be inferred without a separate mapping.
